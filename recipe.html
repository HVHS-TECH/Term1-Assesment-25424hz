Preparation:

  1. 🥩 Meat
    • Prepare 300g pork tenderloin or pork belly
    • Cut into 2-3 cm bite-sized pieces
    • Add:
      • ½ tsp salt
      • A pinch of white pepper
      • 1 tbsp cooking wine
    • Mix well and marinate for 10-15 minutes.
    • Take out another bowl:
      • 1 egg
      • 3 tbsp cornstarch
      • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
    • Add the pork, then stir until the pork is evenly coated and slightly thick.


  2. 🍍 Vegetables
    • ½ onion (cut into chunks)
    • 100-150g pineapple chunks (fresh or canned)

    Tip: Soak pineapple in lightly salted water for 5-10 minutes

    • 1 red capsicum and 1 green capsicum (not necessary)


  3. 🍯 Sauce
    • 4 tbsp ketchup
    • 2 tbsp white vinegar or 2 tbsp rice vinegar or 1.5 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    • 3 tbsp sugar
    • 1 tsp soy sauce
    • 5 tbsp water
